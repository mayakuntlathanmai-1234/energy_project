🌍 Background

Crushing and grinding are vital in mining but consume up to 50% of total energy. This causes high costs and more pollution. The goal is to use smart technologies to save energy and reduce environmental impact.

⚙️ The Problem

  • ⚡ Excessive energy usage
  • 🧰 Increased maintenance costs
  • 📉 Reduced throughput
  • 🌫️ Higher carbon emissions
